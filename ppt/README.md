## 🆘 Troubleshooting

**Presentation won't open:**
- Use Chrome or Firefox browser
- Try: `open -a "Google Chrome" presentation.html`

**Navigation not working:**
- Click on slide area first
- Refresh page (F5)

**Demo fails during presentation:**
- Have recorded video as backup
- Or show screenshots
- Explain what would happen

**Questions you can't answer:**
- Be honest: "Great question, I'll follow up"
- Take note and respond later
- Don't guess or make up answers
